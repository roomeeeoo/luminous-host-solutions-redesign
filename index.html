What we do

Three kinds of work, done to one standard

Most of our work is turnovers. The rest is the deeper, less frequent work that keeps a property from slowly drifting away from the photographs in its listing.

  1. 01

    Short-term rental turnovers

    Thoughtful preparation between one guest and the next. We handle laundry, change linens, remake beds, clean kitchens and bathrooms, and reset each space for a welcoming arrival. Coordinated with your checkout and check-in schedule, every turnover helps keep your property consistent, well presented, and ready for guests.

  2. 02

    Deep cleaning

    Detailed care that reaches beyond routine cleaning. We address stubborn buildup and areas that need periodic, more intensive attention — from shower soap scum and grout to baseboards, door frames, and windowsills. A thorough refresh that helps maintain your property’s condition and brings attention back to the details.

  3. 03

    Move-in and move-out cleaning

    A fresh start for the next chapter. We thoroughly clean empty properties after a move or before a new occupant arrives, giving kitchens, bathrooms, cabinets, and living spaces the attention they need. Each cleaning is tailored to the property’s size and condition, with clear, project-based pricing.

How it works

Four steps, and you only do the first one

  1. 1

    Tell us about the property

    Where it is, how many bedrooms and bathrooms, and how often you need it. The form at the bottom of this page is enough to start.

  2. 2

    We see it before we quote

    We walk the property first, so the quote reflects your property rather than an average of everybody else’s.

  3. 3

    We agree on a standard in writing

    Your property gets its own checklist, not a generic one. Anything particular about it — a stiff lock, a linen closet, the way the sofa bed goes back — is written down so it is done the same way every time.

  4. 4

    We work from your calendar

    We take your booking calendar, so turnovers are scheduled against real checkouts and the house is ready before the next arrival. You are told when it is done.

Questions

Before you ask

Something not covered here? Email us and you will get a straight answer.

What areas do you cover?

Louisville, Kentucky and Southern Indiana. If you are near the edge of that, ask — we will give you a straight answer.

Do you only work with short-term rentals?

Turnovers are most of what we do, but not all of it. We also take deep cleans and move-in or move-out work, including for properties that are not rentals.

What is included in my cleaning?

The scope depends on the service you book and your property’s needs. After inspecting the property, we outline what is included in your quote and service agreement, so you know what to expect before cleaning begins.

How do I get a quote?

Use the form at the bottom of this page. We will come back to you to arrange seeing the property, and quote once we have.

Why do you need to see the property first?

Because properties differ more than their bedroom counts suggest. A quote given without seeing a property is a guess, and guesses get corrected later, which is unpleasant for everybody.

How do I schedule a cleaning?

For short-term rental turnovers, we connect your booking calendar to our cleaning app to coordinate cleanings around guest checkouts and check-ins. Once connected, calendar updates help us keep your turnover schedule aligned with your reservations. For deep cleaning and move-in or move-out cleaning, contact us with your property details and preferred date. We’ll confirm the scope, provide a quote, and schedule your service.

Will it be the same people each time?

Wherever we can, yes. A cleaner who already knows a property does better work in it, so we keep properties with the same people.

Are you insured?

Yes. Luminous Host Solutions and all members of our cleaning team carry general liability insurance. Certificates of insurance are available upon request.

Do you bring cleaning supplies and linens?

Cleaning products are ours — we bring them with us, so there is nothing for you to keep stocked. Laundry products and linens stay at the property and are yours. We tell you through the app when something is running low, so you are not finding out from a guest.

How much notice do you need?

There is no fixed notice period. Send us a quote request and we will tell you honestly what we can take on and when.

What if I need to cancel or reschedule a cleaning?

For deep cleaning and move-in or move-out cleaning, cancellations require at least 24 hours’ notice to avoid a charge. Cancellations with less than 24 hours’ notice are charged in full because that time has been reserved for your property. For short-term rental turnovers, scheduling follows your booking calendar. If a guest cancels or changes their reservation, we adjust the cleaning accordingly at no charge. As long as the change appears on your shared calendar, there is no need to contact us separately.
