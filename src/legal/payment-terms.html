Payment Terms

Last updated November 9, 2026

These terms explain how we price, invoice and take payment. They sit alongside our Terms of Service, and alongside the written service agreement for your property, which governs anything the two disagree about.

Pricing

We price per property, not per hour. The rate for a property is agreed in writing after we have seen it, and reflects its size, layout, and what preparing it actually involves.

If what a property needs changes materially — more bedrooms in use, a different standard, a change in how often it turns over — we will agree a revised rate with you in writing before it applies.

What is billed

We bill for work that has been completed. A job that was not finished is not invoiced.

Any additional charge outside the agreed rate appears as its own line on the invoice, with the reason written out, so you can see exactly what it is for. Discounts appear the same way, as their own line.

Invoicing

Our billing week runs Thursday to Wednesday. Each Thursday we issue one invoice per owner covering the work completed in the week just ended, listing the date of each cleaning, the property, the service, and the amount.

You can ask us for a statement covering any date range at any time.

When payment is due

Invoices are due on receipt. We allow a two-day grace period after the invoice is issued.

If an invoice is still unpaid after that, scheduled cleanings are suspended until the account is brought up to date. We will always tell you before that happens — nobody finds out because a property was not cleaned.

Accepted payment methods are set out in your service agreement.

Questions about an invoice

If something on an invoice looks wrong, tell us before it is paid and we will look into it. We would far rather correct an invoice than have you pay one you disagree with.

Cancellation and rescheduling

Deep cleaning and move-in or move-out cleaning

Cancel or reschedule with at least 24 hours’ notice at no charge. With less than 24 hours’ notice, the cleaning is charged in full because that time has been reserved for your property.

Short-term rental turnovers

Turnover scheduling follows your shared booking calendar. If a guest changes or cancels their reservation, we adjust or cancel the cleaning at no charge, with no advance notice required. As long as the change appears on your shared calendar, there is no need to contact us separately.

Changes

We may update these terms. Changes do not apply retrospectively to work already carried out or to invoices already issued.
