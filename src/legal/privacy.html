Privacy Policy

Last updated November 13, 2026

Luminous Host Solutions LLC (“LHS,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) is a Kentucky limited liability company providing cleaning and property care services.

This Privacy Policy explains how we handle personal information through our website, luminoushostsolutions.com, and the LHS mobile app. It also covers information provided to us in connection with inquiries, property inspections, and service coordination.

For privacy questions or requests, contact hello@luminoushostsolutions.com.

Information collected through our website

Information you provide

When you request a quotation, we collect the information you submit. This may include:

  • Your name, email address, and phone number.
  • Your property’s location.
  • The service you are requesting.
  • The number of bedrooms and bathrooms.
  • Whether you need one-time or recurring service.
  • Your preferred start date.
  • Any additional information you include.

If you contact us by email, we receive your email address, message, attachments, and any other information you provide.

Please do not submit door codes, financial account information, identification documents, or other sensitive information through the public quote form.

Technical information

Our website host and other providers may process technical information necessary to deliver the website, handle requests, and protect against spam or misuse. This may include IP addresses, browser information, request timestamps, and security logs.

We do not use advertising networks, advertising pixels, or website analytics trackers operated by LHS.

Quote form processing

Web3Forms processes quote requests on our behalf and forwards them to our email.

Web3Forms and its infrastructure providers process submission information to deliver the form service and protect against spam and abuse. This may include processing submitters’ IP addresses and email addresses through spam-prevention services.

Web3Forms states that submission data may be retained for up to three years, unless a shorter period applies to our plan or the information is deleted earlier. Copies delivered to our email are subject to LHS’s separate retention practices.

For details, see the Web3Forms Privacy Policy.

Website providers

Our website uses:

  • Netlify for website hosting.
  • Web3Forms for quote form processing and delivery.
  • Google Workspace for email.
  • Google Fonts to deliver website typefaces.

Loading externally hosted fonts sends your IP address and related request information to Google.

Cookies and similar technologies

We do not use cookies for targeted advertising or tracking visitors across unrelated websites.

Technical information may still be processed when you visit the website, even without advertising cookies. Providers may use technologies necessary to deliver or secure their services.

The app uses authentication mechanisms to maintain authorized account access. These serve a different purpose from advertising tracking.

Information collected through the LHS app

The app is invitation-only. LHS creates accounts for authorized users, including independent cleaning contractors, property owners, property managers, and administrators. There is no public self-service registration.

Depending on your role and use of the app, we process the following information.

Account information

  • Name.
  • Email address.
  • Phone number.
  • Preferred language.
  • Assigned role and access permissions.
  • Authentication information needed to manage account access.

Property information

  • Property addresses.
  • Door codes, lockbox codes, and other entry instructions.
  • Property notes and cleaning requirements.
  • Information needed to coordinate cleaning services.

Property addresses identify where services take place. They are not obtained by tracking a user’s device location.

Job and activity records

We maintain records of scheduled and completed work, including:

  • Job assignments and scheduling changes.
  • Who performed recorded actions and when.
  • Job start and completion activity.
  • Completed tasks and reported concerns.
  • Calendar synchronization activity.
  • Notification records.

Booking calendar information

We process booking dates from calendars shared or connected for cleaning coordination.

A calendar feed may contain additional reservation information. Our scheduling records do not retain guest names, guest email addresses, or guest phone numbers extracted from those feeds.

Photographs

We process photographs to document completed work, property condition, and reported concerns.

Photographs may be taken within the app or selected from a device’s photo library.

When a photograph is selected from the photo library, the app reads available metadata to determine when it was taken. Only the capture timestamp is retained from that metadata. Uploaded photographs are re-encoded without the original embedded metadata, including any GPS metadata.

The app does not use photo metadata to track a user’s location.

Photographs are stored privately and made available to authorized people for service documentation and coordination. They are not published for advertising without separate permission.

Notifications

If notifications are enabled, we process notification identifiers, such as device push tokens, and notification content to deliver service-related messages. Notification records are also stored within our systems.

You can manage push notification permissions through your device settings. Turning off notifications does not automatically deactivate your account.

Crash and diagnostic information

We use Sentry to help identify and resolve app errors and crashes.

Diagnostic information may include technical details about the device, app version, error, and activity associated with a failure.

We use filtering designed to remove confidential values, including door codes, Wi-Fi passwords, and private calendar URLs, from diagnostic reports. These safeguards reduce exposure but do not constitute a guarantee that every report will be free of personal information.

Device location

The app does not request device-location permission or collect device location, either while in use or in the background.

App payments

Invoice payments are not currently available through the app. Before introducing that feature, we will update this policy to explain the payment provider and how payment-related information is handled.

How we use information

We use information to:

  • Respond to inquiries and arrange property inspections.
  • Prepare quotations and administer client relationships.
  • Coordinate and perform cleaning services.
  • Manage authorized app accounts and permissions.
  • Schedule work using shared booking calendars.
  • Document completed work and address service concerns.
  • Send service-related notifications and communications.
  • Diagnose technical problems and protect our systems.
  • Maintain accounting, tax, contractor, and business records.
  • Address disputes, insurance matters, and legal obligations.

We do not sell or rent personal information or use it for targeted advertising.

Who can access information

Access depends on a person’s role and legitimate need for the information.

LHS administrators may access information necessary to operate the business and manage services. Assigned cleaning contractors receive information needed to perform their work, including relevant property and entry instructions.

Owners and authorized property representatives receive information relevant to their properties and services.

Access to owner pricing, billing information, and contractor information is restricted according to assigned permissions and business responsibilities.

Users must not use or disclose confidential information for purposes unrelated to their authorized work or services.

Service providers and other disclosures

We use service providers to operate our website, app, and communications, including:

  • Netlify: website hosting.
  • Web3Forms: quote form processing and delivery.
  • Google Workspace: email.
  • Google Fonts: website font delivery.
  • Supabase: app database, authentication, and photograph storage.
  • Expo: push notification delivery.
  • Sentry: crash reporting and diagnostics.
  • Apple and Google notification services: delivery of push notifications to supported devices.

These providers may use infrastructure providers and subprocessors to perform their services.

We may also disclose relevant information when reasonably necessary to comply with law, respond to lawful requests, protect people or property, investigate misuse, or establish, exercise, or defend legal claims. This may include appropriate disclosures to legal advisers, insurers, or authorities.

We do not make private property information or app photographs publicly available merely because they are stored in our systems.

Information security

We use access controls and other technical and organizational measures designed to protect information against unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or loss.

Entry instructions and similar confidential information receive restricted access based on authorized roles and assignments.

No electronic system or transmission method is completely secure. Keep your account credentials private and notify us promptly if you suspect unauthorized access or disclosure.

Retention and deletion

Inquiries and correspondence

We retain inquiries and related correspondence while responding to them and for a reasonable period afterward, taking into account ongoing conversations, service relationships, recordkeeping needs, and applicable legal obligations.

Web3Forms maintains its own retention practices as described above. Deleting an email copy does not automatically delete information held by Web3Forms, and deleting a Web3Forms submission does not automatically delete our email copy.

App photographs

Photographs stored in the app are retained for 90 days and then permanently deleted.

Our database backups do not include the photograph storage objects. Once those photographs are deleted from our storage, LHS cannot restore them from its database backups.

This process does not delete original photographs remaining in a user’s device photo library or copies independently downloaded or retained by another authorized recipient.

Accounts and other app records

LHS currently manages account access through administrator-controlled deactivation. Deactivation prevents further authorized account use but does not delete the profile or associated historical records.

Profiles are not automatically deleted when a working relationship ends. Account, job, activity, and other database records may remain in our database and its backups.

We retain business records to support service administration, tax and accounting obligations, contractor records, security, and the handling of claims or disputes. Privacy requests concerning retained information are reviewed as described below.

Privacy requests and choices

You may contact us to:

  • Ask about the personal information we hold about you.
  • Request correction of inaccurate information.
  • Request deletion of information.
  • Request account deactivation.
  • Ask questions about our collection, use, or retention practices.

Send requests to hello@luminoushostsolutions.com.

We may need to verify your identity and authority before disclosing or changing information. We will respond within the period required by applicable law.

There is currently no self-service account deletion function in the app. A deletion request is reviewed separately from account deactivation and does not automatically result in deletion of all records.

We may retain information when required by law or reasonably necessary for accounting, security, ongoing services, or legal claims, subject to applicable law. Where a request cannot be fully fulfilled, we will explain the applicable limitations.

Your legal rights may depend on your location and whether a particular privacy law applies to LHS. Nothing in this policy limits rights available under applicable law.

Processing locations

LHS is based in Kentucky, but our providers may process or store information in the United States and other countries.

For example, Web3Forms operates from India and uses infrastructure in multiple regions. Information may therefore be processed outside your state or country of residence.

Children

App accounts are intended for adults aged 18 or older. Our website and services are not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, contact us so we can investigate and take appropriate steps, including deletion where required.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as our services, technology, or legal requirements change. The date at the top identifies the latest update.

For material changes, we will provide additional notice through the website, app, email, or another appropriate method. Where required by law, we will obtain consent before applying a change.
